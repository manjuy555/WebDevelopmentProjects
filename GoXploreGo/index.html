  • Safety Advisory
  • Check daily weather and rainfall forecasts, as heavy showers often trigger landslides and road closures between Shillong, Cherrapunji, Dawki, and Mawsynram.
  • Carry rain jackets even in winter, since sudden drizzles occur frequently in Meghalaya’s cloud-heavy climate.
  • Wear sturdy footwear for treks to living root bridges, as stone steps remain slippery year-round due to moisture.
  • Follow village council instructions carefully, as access rules differ across Khasi, Jaintia, and Garo regions.
  • Hire a local guide for the Double Decker Living Root Bridge trek in Nongriat, as the 3,000+ stone steps become slippery after rainfall.
  • Avoid trekking remote trails alone, and hire village guides for safe navigation and river crossings.
